Every account, personal or organization, has its own plan: **FREE**, **PRO** or **ENTERPRISE**. Plans are flat-rate, billed monthly through Stripe. Nothing is metered, so there is no charge per request. What each plan allows is on [Plans and limits](/reference/plans-and-limits).

The **Billing** link is in the sidebar for **owners** of an organization; a personal account has no Billing link in the sidebar. Other members can open the page by its address and see the plan, but the invoice history is for owners only ("Invoice history is only visible to organization owners.").

## The plan card

**Current plan** shows a badge for the plan and, once you have a subscription, a second one for its status, such as `TRIALING`, `ACTIVE`, `PAST_DUE` or `CANCELED`. For a paid plan it also shows **Next billing date** (or **Cancels on**, if the subscription is set to end) and **Trial ends** if you are on a trial.

## Upgrade

On the FREE plan the card has an **Upgrade** button.

1. Choose **Upgrade** and pick **Pro** or **Enterprise**. The dialog shows each plan's monthly price.
2. Choose **Continue to checkout**. You are sent to Stripe's payment page; VhyxVoid never sees your card details.
3. After paying you come back to **Billing** with a banner: "Plan upgraded successfully. Welcome to your new plan!"

The plan applies to the account you upgraded from, not to your other accounts.

## Manage or cancel

With a paid plan, **Manage subscription** opens Stripe's customer portal, where you update the payment method, see invoices, and cancel or resume. When a subscription is set to cancel, the card shows a warning: "Your subscription will cancel at end of the current period."

## Invoices

Owners see **Invoice history**. Each row has the billing period (or "One-time payment"), a status (`PAID`, `OPEN`, `DRAFT`, `UNCOLLECTIBLE` or `VOID`), the date it was paid, and the amount. A **PDF** button downloads the invoice, and an unpaid invoice has a **Pay now** button.

## When a payment fails

When Stripe reports a failed payment:

1. The subscription and the account move to `PAST_DUE`, and the owner is emailed. The plan card turns red with "Payment failed. Please update your payment method to avoid service interruption." and an **Update payment** button that opens the Stripe portal.
2. Your plan's limits stay in force for a **seven day grace period**.
3. If it isn't resolved within that time, the account is `SUSPENDED` (a check runs hourly) and gets the FREE plan's limits.

<Callout type="info" title="Fixed in source, not yet deployed">
  Until a fix dated 2026-09-22 is deployed, step 2's seven-day deadline is never actually recorded, so step 3's scheduled check finds nothing to suspend and a `PAST_DUE` account stays `PAST_DUE` indefinitely. This is being corrected server-side; nothing to do on your end. Read from the code, not tested against a real overdue account.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Agents need an active account to connect">
  The hub only accepts an agent's connection when the account's status is `ACTIVE`. An agent that connects, or has to reconnect, while the account is `PAST_DUE` or `SUSPENDED` is refused with `Account is not active` and stops. The grace period protects your plan's limits, not your ability to connect. This comes from reading the current hub code, not from a test with a real overdue account. Fix the payment method first, then restart the agent.
</Callout>

**While the account is `PAST_DUE`**, paying takes it back to `ACTIVE` automatically. **Once it reaches `SUSPENDED`**, paying alone no longer does: reactivating a suspended account is a deliberate step, not something a Stripe event does on its own (fixed 2026-09-22, not yet deployed — the account status doesn't currently record *why* it was suspended, so nothing can safely tell "suspended for non-payment" apart from any other kind of suspension). If your account is `SUSPENDED`, updating your payment method is still worth doing, but it won't reactivate the account by itself. You can see the account's status in the **My organizations** table on the [dashboard](/dashboard/account-and-organizations).
