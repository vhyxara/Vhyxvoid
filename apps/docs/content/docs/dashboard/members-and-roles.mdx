An organization has three roles. The **Members** page lists everyone in it and is where you manage them. Admins and owners can also open **Settings**, which has the same table on a **Members** tab and an **Invitations** tab.

## The three roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can, plus billing and invoices, and transferring ownership. |
| **Admin** | Create API keys, invite members, rename the organization, and manage members below them. Sees every key. |
| **Member** | See the organization, its tunnels and the members, and use the keys they created. |

A person can only manage people ranked below them, and can only give roles below their own. So an admin can make someone a member, and only the owner can make someone an admin.

## The members table

Each row shows the person's name and email (with a **You** badge on your own row), whether their email is **Verified** or **Pending**, their **Role**, and when they **Joined**. You can search, filter by role, and sort by role or joined date.

The action buttons on a row are:

| Button | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shield | **Change role** | For people you can manage. Never on your own row. |
| Crown | **Transfer ownership** | Only for the owner, on rows that aren't the owner. |
| Trash | **Remove member** | For people you can manage. Never on your own row. |

If a button is greyed out, the server decided you can't manage that person.

## Invite someone

Admins and owners see **Invite member**.

1. Choose **Invite member**, enter their email address, and choose a role. The list only offers roles you're allowed to give: **Member** or **Admin** for an owner, and **Member** for an admin.
2. Choose **Send invite**. VhyxVoid emails them a link.

The **Invitations** tab under **Settings** (admins and owners only) shows every invitation with the email, role, who sent it, when it expires, and its status (`PENDING` and others). A pending invitation has a button to cancel it. An invitation link is valid for **three days**.

### Accepting an invitation

The invited person opens the link in the email. They need to be signed in with the account that has the **same email address** the invitation was sent to; otherwise it fails with `Invitation email does not match user`. On success they see "You're in!" and are taken to their dashboard, where the organization now appears. An expired or already-used link shows an error, and you can send a new invitation.

## Change a role

Choose the shield button, pick the new role from the list, and choose **Save**. The dialog offers the roles you may assign, which are always below your own.

## Transfer ownership

Only the owner can do this. Choose the crown button on the person who should become the owner and confirm **Transfer ownership**.

<Callout type="warn">
  There is no second confirmation step. One click completes it: they become the Owner and you become an Admin, so you can't reverse it yourself.
</Callout>

## Remove a member

Choose the trash button and confirm **Remove**. The dialog says "This cannot be undone": to bring the person back you have to invite them again.
