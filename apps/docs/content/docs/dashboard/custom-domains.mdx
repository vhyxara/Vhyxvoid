Instead of `acme--web.vhyxvoid.com`, a tunnel can answer on a hostname you own, like `api.yourcompany.com`. Everything works the same on it: [access rules](/docs/dashboard/tunnel-access), the [request inspector](/docs/dashboard/request-inspector) and the [webhook inbox](/docs/dashboard/webhook-inbox).

## Add a domain

1. Open **Domains** under a workspace, enter the hostname and pick the tunnel (by its `--label`). Owners and admins can do this.
2. Create the two DNS records shown, where your domain's DNS is hosted (Cloudflare, Route 53, your registrar):

| Record | Name | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `TXT` | `_vhyxvoid.<your hostname>` | `vhyxvoid-verify=…` | proves you own the domain |
| `CNAME` | `<your hostname>` | the target shown on the page | sends visitors to us |

3. That's it. We check DNS every few minutes (or press **Check DNS now**). When both records are found the domain shows **active**, and the HTTPS certificate is issued automatically on the first visit, which takes a few seconds.

For a root domain (`yourcompany.com`, no subdomain), DNS does not allow a CNAME. Use your provider's ALIAS or "CNAME flattening" record pointing at the same target; we accept any record whose addresses match ours.

If you use Cloudflare's proxy (orange cloud), turn it off for this record (DNS only): the certificate is issued by us, so Cloudflare must pass the connection through.

## States

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| waiting for DNS | The TXT record has not been seen yet. Nothing is served on the hostname. |
| verified, DNS not pointing here | Ownership is proven, but the hostname does not point at us (yet, or any more). |
| active | Requests to the hostname reach the tunnel. |

The **Tunnel** picker on a domain moves it to another tunnel immediately. **Remove** stops serving it immediately.

## Good to know

- A hostname can only be active in one workspace. Someone else can add it to theirs, but only the first to prove ownership with the TXT record gets it.
- Your plan sets how many custom domains you can have. Domains you already have keep working if your plan changes.
- Turn on an alert of type **Custom domain changes** to hear when a domain is verified, or stops pointing at us (for example after someone edits DNS). See [alerts](/docs/dashboard/alerts).
