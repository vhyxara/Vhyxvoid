This page describes the dashboard as it works today, from the code of the dashboard itself. Screens change; where a button or label matters, the page quotes it.

## Finding your way around

After you sign in you land on **Dashboard**, a table called **My organizations**. Every account you belong to has a row:

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | The name (a personal account shows as **Personal account**), and under it the type (`PERSONAL` or `ORGANIZATION`) and status (for example `ACTIVE`). |
| Your role | Owner, Admin or Member. |
| Joined | When you joined. |
| Actions | An arrow that opens the account. |

You can search the table, filter it by role, and sort it. The arrow opens that account's **Members** page.

The sidebar on the left has **Dashboard**, then **Organizations**, then **My profile**. Under **Organizations**, each organization you belong to expands into these links:

| Link | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| Members | Everyone in the organization |
| API Keys | Everyone in the organization |
| Tunnels | Everyone in the organization |
| Billing | Owners |
| Settings | Admins and owners |

An Owner or Admin badge appears next to the organization's name in the sidebar.

<Callout type="info" title="Your personal account isn't in the sidebar">
  The sidebar lists organizations only. If you don't belong to one, it shows a **Create organization** link instead, and you have no sidebar links to API keys or tunnels until you create one. Your personal account is still in the **My organizations** table, and its arrow opens it.
</Callout>

## Personal accounts and organizations

There are two kinds of account. A **personal account** is yours alone. An **organization** is shared: you can invite people, give them roles, and share its keys, tunnels and billing. Plans and billing belong to the account, so each organization has its own plan. See [Members and roles](/dashboard/members-and-roles) for what each role can do.

## Create an organization

Anyone can create one.

1. On **Dashboard**, choose **New organization**.
2. Enter a name of 2 to 64 characters (for example `Acme Corp`) and choose **Create**.

You become its Owner, and the dashboard opens it. It starts on the FREE plan; see [Billing](/dashboard/billing).

## Rename an organization

Admins and owners can rename an organization. Open **Settings** in the sidebar, edit **Organization name** under **Rename organization**, and choose **Save**. The same page shows the member count and the number of active API keys, and holds the **Members** and **Invitations** tabs described in [Members and roles](/dashboard/members-and-roles).

## Your profile

**My profile** shows your name and whether your email is verified. You can edit your first and last name; the email address is read-only. It also lists your organization memberships, a form to change your password, and your [feedback history](/dashboard/feedback-and-notifications#your-feedback-history).
