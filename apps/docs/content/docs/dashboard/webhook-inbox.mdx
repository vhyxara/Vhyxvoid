Webhook providers give up on endpoints that keep failing. Without an inbox, a webhook sent while your laptop is asleep or your agent is stopped gets `404` and may never come back. With the inbox on, VhyxVoid keeps it and hands it to your app as soon as the tunnel is online again.

## Turn it on

Open **Webhook inbox** under a workspace and turn it on for a tunnel (by its `--label`). Owners and admins can change it.

## While the tunnel is offline

- `POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH` and `DELETE` requests are stored and answered with `202 Accepted` and an `x-vhyxvoid-inbox` header carrying the stored request's id. Providers treat that as success and stop retrying.
- `GET` and other reads still get `404`, as before.
- Access rules still apply: a password-protected tunnel only stores requests that pass the password or IP check.
- A [mock API](/dashboard/mock-apis) on the same label answers first; only requests it has no endpoint for are stored.
- Bodies up to 1 MB are kept. Larger requests get `413`.
- Your plan sets how many requests can wait per tunnel. When it is full, further requests get `503` with `Retry-After`, so the provider retries later.

## When the agent reconnects

Waiting requests are delivered oldest first, with their original method, path, headers and body, through the tunnel to your app. Each delivery also shows up in the [request inspector](/dashboard/request-inspector), marked as coming from the inbox.

- Any answer from your app below `500` counts as delivered (a `4xx` is shown so you can fix your handler and **Redeliver**).
- A `5xx`, or your local server not answering, is retried after 30 s, 1 min, 2 min… up to 8 attempts, then marked **failed**. Requests behind it wait, so they never overtake it.
- If the tunnel goes offline again mid-way, delivery stops and continues on the next reconnect; that does not count as an attempt.

Signed webhooks: providers that put a timestamp in the signature (Stripe, for example) reject old deliveries by default. Increase your handler's tolerance while testing, or redeliver from the provider's dashboard.

## Managing it

Filter by **Waiting**, **Delivered** or **Failed**, open a request to see its headers (credentials hidden) and body, **Deliver now** or **Redeliver** one, or delete them. Everything is deleted automatically 7 days after it arrived.
