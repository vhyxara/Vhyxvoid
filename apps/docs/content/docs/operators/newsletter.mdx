Every public page of the website ends with a sign-up band above the footer: a title, a line of text and an email field. Its words are **Settings › Newsletter** (*Form title*, *Form text*); *Newsletter sign-up* switches it off, which hides the band and refuses new sign-ups (existing subscribers stay).

## Double opt-in

Signing up doesn't subscribe anyone yet. The address gets an email with a confirmation link, and only a confirmed address receives anything:

1. A visitor types their address and clicks **Subscribe**. The band says to check the inbox.
2. The email ("Confirm your subscription…", wording editable on the [Emails](/operators/emails) screen) links to `/newsletter/confirm`. The link works for 7 days.
3. Opening it confirms the subscription.

Protections:

- The answer is the same whether or not the address is already subscribed, so the form can't be used to find out who is.
- At most one confirmation email per address every 10 minutes, and 5 sign-ups per 10 minutes per visitor, so nobody can flood someone's inbox.
- A hidden field catches bots.

## Unsubscribing

Every newsletter email has an **Unsubscribe** link to `/newsletter/unsubscribe`, which asks for one click (so a link preview can't unsubscribe anyone). The emails also carry the standard one-click unsubscribe headers, so Gmail, Apple Mail and others show their own *Unsubscribe* button. Links in emails are signed, so they can't be guessed.

## Subscribers

**Newsletter** in the console lists every address with its status (*Subscribed*, *Not confirmed*, *Unsubscribed*), where on the site they signed up and when, with counts at the top.

- **Export subscribers (CSV)** downloads the confirmed addresses, for example to move them to a mailing service. Exports are in the admin audit log.
- **Delete** removes an address for good, for requests to erase someone's data. To only stop the emails, they can unsubscribe.

## Emailing a post

In a blog post's **Publishing** tab, **Email to subscribers** sends the post's title, excerpt and cover image, with a button to read it, to every confirmed subscriber:

- **Send me a test** sends it to your own address first.
- **Email N subscribers…** asks for confirmation, then sends. A post can be sent once; the tab then shows when.
- Only a live post can be sent, with no unsaved changes.

Emails go out one at a time in the background (under two a second, to stay within the email provider's limits; `NEWSLETTER_SEND_INTERVAL_MS` on the API changes the pause). If the API restarts while sending, the rest of that send is not resumed.

## Who can do what

| Ability | Allows |
| --- | --- |
| `newsletter.read` | View and export subscribers |
| `newsletter.update` | Delete subscribers |
| `newsletter.send` | Email a live blog post to subscribers |
