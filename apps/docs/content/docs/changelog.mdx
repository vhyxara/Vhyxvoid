## October 2026

**Dashboard**

- **Traffic rules**: per-tunnel mocks, injected errors and latency, redirects, path rewrites and header changes, applied before requests reach your machine, plus maintenance answers while the agent is offline. See [Traffic rules](/docs/dashboard/traffic-rules).
- **Traffic charts** on the Tunnels page and the overview: requests per minute, 15 minutes or 2 hours, split into successful, `4xx` and `5xx`, with error rate and average answer time per tunnel. See [Tunnels](/docs/dashboard/tunnels#traffic).
- **Activity**: every change in a workspace (members, keys, access rules, domains, alerts, inbox) and every tunnel connect and disconnect, with CSV export for owners and admins. See [Activity](/docs/dashboard/activity).
- **Usage and trial notices**: owners and admins hear about 80% and 100% of the monthly requests and about a trial ending, once each. See [Alerts](/docs/dashboard/alerts#usage-and-trial-notices).
- **Request capture switch**: a workspace can stop the request inspector from storing anything. See [Request inspector](/docs/dashboard/request-inspector#turning-capture-off).
- Earlier this month: [request inspector and replay](/docs/dashboard/request-inspector), [tunnel access rules](/docs/dashboard/tunnel-access), the [webhook inbox](/docs/dashboard/webhook-inbox), [custom domains](/docs/dashboard/custom-domains) and [alerts](/docs/dashboard/alerts).

**CLI and CI**

- `vhyxvoid doctor` checks your key, local server, hub, sign-in and custom domain DNS, and tells you how to fix what is wrong. See [doctor](/docs/cli/doctor).
- A [GitHub Action](/docs/integrations/github-actions) gives a CI job a public URL and comments it on the pull request.

**Security**

- After 10 wrong tunnel passwords in a minute from one address, the hub answers `429` for the rest of that minute.
- Sign-ins expire after 90 days (dashboard) and 30 days (admin console) however often they refresh. Admin accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 wrong passwords.
